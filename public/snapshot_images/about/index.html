About

Hi, I’m Olivia. At my core, I have deep passion for robotics engineering in all aspects - whether that falls within autonomy software, hardware integration, or sensors & electrical design. My current rabbit hole: sensors & autonomy software, and deep learning as it relates to robotics.

Over the course of my degree, I’ve done a little bit of everything, and I’m always open to chat. Hit me up about anything engineering - or, running a half-marathon, doing a triathlon (one day!), and classical music, just some of my recent interests! You can find me at olivia.ye@uwaterloo.ca.

Previously, I’ve worked at Medra, where I was able to write motion control software for a 7-DOF robotic arm both in real life and simulation. At Zoox, I worked on hardware systems integration, getting my hands deep into the nitty gritty parts of autonomous vehicles. At WHOOP and Verkada, I delved into the consumer electronics space, designing, validating, and shipping electrical hardware for wearables and physical security on a scale of thousands to a millions of units.

I’ve also worked on many projects related to mechatronics systems, perception, ML, and more, from quick weekend hackathons to multi-year space projects. I see my ability to understand all components of a mechatronics system and agility in working with small and nimble teams to large complex organizations as one of my greatest strengths.
